I'm starting this blog as a public notebook — partly for myself (writing forces clarity), partly because I'm in the final stretch of an M.S. in Remote Sensing at NCU and currently looking for fully-remote ML/CV/Earth Observation roles.

## What I'm working on right now

My thesis sits at the intersection of **synthetic aperture radar (SAR) image processing** and **deep-learning object detection**: specifically, comparing how different speckle-noise reduction techniques (Lee, Refined Lee, Frost filters) affect ship detection accuracy. The proposed Lee-filter integration into `torchgeo` ([PR #3655](https://github.com/torchgeo/torchgeo/pull/3655)) is the first step toward making these preprocessing tools routinely available in the PyTorch geospatial ecosystem.
